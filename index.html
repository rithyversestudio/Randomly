Random Number Generator

Randomly is an online random number generator. Set a minimum and maximum, and generate a random number instantly, from 1 to 100 or all the way up to 1,000,000.

How to Generate a Random Number

  1. Enter the minimum number.
  2. Enter the maximum number.
  3. Choose a reveal animation.
  4. Click Reveal Random Number.

You'll get a random whole number within your range.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a random number generator?

A tool that picks a number with no predictable pattern. Randomly picks a whole number between a minimum and maximum you choose.

How does a random number generator work?

Randomly uses your browser's built-in cryptographic random source, so every whole number in your range has an equal chance.

Can I generate a random number between two numbers?

Yes. Enter any minimum and maximum from 1 to 1,000,000. Both endpoints can be picked.

Can I generate a random number from 1 to 100?

Yes. Tap the 1–100 quick range, then reveal your number.

Can I generate a random number from 1 to 1,000,000?

Yes. Tap the 1–1,000,000 quick range to get a whole number up to one million.

Is Randomly free to use?

Yes. It's free and works in your browser with no sign-up.
